I am a clinical researcher bridging the gap between clinical practice and computational biology. My work focuses on the intersection of **Oral Medicine, Pharmacology, and Oral Surgery**, utilizing high-throughput data science to translate clinical challenges into predictive, precision-medicine solutions.

**🔬 Core Research Interests:**
* **Oral Medicine & Pathology:** Investigating the tumor microenvironment, mucosal diseases, and head/neck oncology.
* **Pharmacology & Therapeutics:** Exploring drug-target interactions, transcriptomic responses, and pharmacogenomic modeling in oral diseases.
* **Surgical Outcomes:** Utilizing clinical data to build independent predictive risk models and improve evidence-based surgical interventions.

**💻 Computational & Analytical Toolkit:**
* **Languages:** R 
* **Bioinformatics:** Bioconductor, transcriptomic data pipelines, immune deconvolution mapping.
* **Machine Learning:** L1-penalized (LASSO) neural networks for predictive biomarker discovery.
* **Statistics:** Multivariate Cox Proportional-Hazards regression, Kaplan-Meier survival analysis, and clinical correlation modeling.
