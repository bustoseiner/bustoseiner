## Who I am

I'm a dental surgeon. My day is split between the chair, the page and the classroom: I operate, I help others do rigorous research, and I teach.

I care about one thing above all: **good decisions made on good evidence.** In surgery, that means knowing why before you cut. In research, it means asking the right question before running the test. In teaching, it means giving students a structure to hang knowledge on, so it doesn't collapse.

## What I believe

> **Without structure, knowledge collapses.**

- **Evidence is a method, not a citation.** Reading a paper is not the same as understanding what it can and cannot tell you.
- **Rigor is a form of respect.** For patients, for readers, and for the people who will build on your work.
- **Clarity is a skill.** Complex ideas should be made digestible, never diluted.
- **Tools serve judgment.** Code, statistics and AI are powerful aids, and the final responsibility always stays human.

## How I work

1. **Start with the question.** A clear question saves months of wrong answers.
2. **Design before data.** Methods are decided before results exist.
3. **Make it reproducible.** If someone else can't follow it, it isn't finished.
4. **Explain it plainly.** If I can't teach it simply, I haven't understood it yet.
